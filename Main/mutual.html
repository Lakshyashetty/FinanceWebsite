What is a Mutual Fund?

A mutual fund pools money from many investors and invests it in stocks, bonds, or other assets. It is managed by professionals and helps you grow wealth over time.

  • ✓ Good for beginners
  • ✓ Low start amount (SIP)
  • ✓ Diversification

Frequently Asked Questions

Is mutual fund safe? ▼
Mutual funds are market-linked. They are safe in structure, but returns depend on market performance.
What is NAV? ▼
NAV means Net Asset Value. It is the price of 1 unit of a mutual fund.
How much should I invest? ▼
Start with what you can afford. Even ₹500/month SIP is a good beginning.
What is SIP? ▼
SIP means Systematic Investment Plan. You invest a fixed amount monthly into a mutual fund.
Can I withdraw anytime? ▼
Most funds allow withdrawal anytime, except ELSS which has a 3-year lock-in.
What is expense ratio? ▼
Expense ratio is the fee charged by the mutual fund company for managing your investment.
