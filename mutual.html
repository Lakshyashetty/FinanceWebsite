Foundation

What is a Mutual Fund?

A mutual fund pools money from many investors and invests it in stocks, bonds, or other assets. It is managed by professionals and helps you build financial exposure in a structured way over time.

  • ✓ Good for beginners
  • ✓ Low start amount with SIP
  • ✓ Diversification across assets

Types of Mutual Funds

Each mutual fund type has a different risk level, return potential, and investment purpose. Understanding the category helps you read the product more clearly.

Equity Funds

Higher return potential with higher risk. Commonly used for long-term growth goals.

Growth

Debt Funds

Typically more stable with lower risk. Often preferred for capital preservation.

Stability

Hybrid Funds

A mix of equity and debt that aims to balance growth and stability.

Balanced

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is mutual fund safe? ▼
Mutual funds are market-linked instruments. Their structure is regulated, but returns and values can move based on market performance.
What is NAV? ▼
NAV means Net Asset Value. It represents the value of one unit of a mutual fund.
How much should I invest? ▼
People usually begin with an amount they can comfortably sustain. Even a small SIP can be useful for learning and discipline.
What is SIP? ▼
SIP means Systematic Investment Plan. It allows a fixed amount to be invested periodically, often monthly.
Can I withdraw anytime? ▼
Many mutual funds allow redemption subject to scheme terms, while some categories such as ELSS come with a lock-in period.
What is expense ratio? ▼
Expense ratio is the fee charged for managing and operating the mutual fund scheme.
